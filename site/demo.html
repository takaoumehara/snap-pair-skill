    How it works

    The host turns its PIN into a channel name starting with snap-pair-demo/; the controller joins the same channel with the same PIN. Pointer positions are normalized to 0–1 and batched once per animation frame. Heartbeats detect disconnects. In a real app BroadcastChannelTransport does all of this for you, and swapping in PartyKitTransport or WebRTCTransport takes it across the internet.
