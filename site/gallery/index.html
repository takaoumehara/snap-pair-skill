How to submit

  1. Build a multi-screen experience with snap-pair-core and deploy it publicly over HTTPS.
  2. Click "Submit your site" above and fill out the form with your site URL, title, and description.
  3. Our automated system will check that your site loads and uses Snap Pair.
  4. If accepted, your site will appear here in the gallery!

We look for evidence of Snap Pair usage in your site's code, scripts, or network requests. If you have a public GitHub repository, we'll also check package.json for snap-pair-core. Sites that bundle or minify heavily may hide these traces; if your submission is incorrectly rejected, a maintainer can manually approve it with the gallery-approve label.
